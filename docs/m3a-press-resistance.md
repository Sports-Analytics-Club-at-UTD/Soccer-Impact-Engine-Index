# Press-resistance measurements

Public method overview. The unchanged original contains historical empirical
examples retained privately; those results are not included here.

The existing ratio is successful pressured passes/dribbles divided by their pressured attempts. Minutes are not that denominator. Zero attempts are unavailable; zero successes over known attempts remain zero. Retain the existing outcome definitions and distinct sample-admission rules.

See [implementation or current method](../src/press_resistance.py),
[the current local application](unified-local-website-integration.md), and
[test inventory/private verification](test-suite-inventory.md).
