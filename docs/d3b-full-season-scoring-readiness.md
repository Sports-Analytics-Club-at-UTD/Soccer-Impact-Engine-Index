# Full-season readiness

Public method overview. The unchanged original contains historical empirical
examples retained privately; those results are not included here.

A declared observed fixture inventory does not prove historical completeness. Readiness needs exact identity, compatible definitions, supported exposure and reviewed evidence for the intended population. Retain unresolved transfers and appearances; do not fit an official pool from incomplete tables.

See [implementation or current method](../scripts/audit_dri_readiness.py),
[the current local application](unified-local-website-integration.md), and
[test inventory/private verification](test-suite-inventory.md).
This summary creates no source attestation, scoring or publication approval.
